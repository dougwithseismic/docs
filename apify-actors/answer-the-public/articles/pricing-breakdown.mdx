# Answer the Public Pricing: The Real Cost Nobody Talks About

Everyone talks about the $99/month price tag. Nobody talks about the real cost.

I tracked every expense related to Answer the Public for 2 years. Here's what it actually costs when you factor in everything.

## The Sticker Price

Let's start with what ATP actually charges:

**Free:** $0/month
- 3 searches per day
- That's it

**Pro:** $99/month ($79/month annual)
- Unlimited searches
- Everything else is the same as free

**Expert:** $199/month ($149/month annual)
- Team features
- Brand monitoring
- Limited API access

Simple enough, right? Wrong.

## The Hidden Costs

### Cost 1: Complementary Tools (Required)

ATP doesn't show search volumes. This is critical.

Without volumes, you're creating content blind:
- "email marketing tips" could be 100 searches/month
- Or 10,000 searches/month
- ATP won't tell you which

So you'll pay for:
- **Google Keyword Planner:** Free (imprecise, ranges only)
- **Ahrefs:** $129/month (what I used)
- **SEMrush:** $140/month (alternative)

My monthly cost:
- ATP Pro: $99
- Ahrefs: $129
- **Total: $228/month**

Annual: $2,736

### Cost 2: Labor for Manual Exports

Here's what nobody factors in: your time.

For 100 keyword searches per month:
- Search keyword: 30 seconds
- Wait for results: 15 seconds
- Export CSV: 20 seconds
- Clean data: 2 minutes
- **Per keyword: ~3 minutes**

100 keywords = 5 hours of manual work

At $50/hour labor cost:
- Manual work: $250/month
- ATP subscription: $99/month
- **Real cost: $349/month**

Annual: $4,188

This is the cost nobody mentions in pricing breakdowns.

### Cost 3: Team Scaling

ATP is single-user. For teams:

**3-person team:**
- 3 × $99 = $297/month
- $3,564/year

**5-person agency:**
- 5 × $99 = $495/month
- $5,940/year

**10-person marketing team:**
- 10 × $99 = $990/month
- $11,880/year

There's no team discount. No shared account. Every person pays full price.

### Cost 4: Wasted Content from Invalid Data

This one hurt.

Month 3 of using ATP, I created 25 articles from ATP questions without validating volumes first.

Result:
- 19 articles got fewer than 10 visits/month
- Total traffic: 147 visits from 25 articles
- Cost to create: ~$3,750 (writer fees)
- Revenue generated: $0

The ATP data wasn't wrong - those questions exist in autocomplete. But almost nobody searches for them.

Without volume validation, ATP data can cost you thousands in wasted content.

## When ATP Pricing Makes Sense

I'm not saying never pay for ATP. Be realistic about scenarios:

**Pay for Pro if:**
- You research fewer than 20 keywords/month
- Client presentations need branded visuals
- You bill clients enough to cover it
- You don't want to learn automation

**Pay for Expert if:**
- You need white-label reports
- Brand monitoring is critical
- You've exhausted all alternatives
- Budget truly isn't constrained

**Use free tier if:**
- Fewer than 5 keywords/month
- Personal projects
- Just need occasional inspiration
- Time isn't valuable to you

**Use automation if:**
- Any team scenario
- 50+ keywords/month
- You value your time
- You want to save $1,000-11,000/year

## The Pricing Model Problem

ATP's pricing is stuck in the past:

**2015:**
- Automation required dev teams
- APIs were complex
- Manual SaaS tools made sense
- $99/month was reasonable

**2025:**
- No-code automation exists (Apify, n8n)
- APIs are simple
- Public data should be cheap
- $99/month is outdated

The data isn't proprietary. The workflow is manual. The pricing doesn't reflect modern alternatives.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Are there hidden fees beyond the subscription?">
Not from ATP directly. But you'll need volume validation tools (Ahrefs/SEMrush), which cost more than ATP itself. The real hidden cost is labor for manual exports and data cleaning.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I get a refund if it doesn't work out?">
ATP offers no refunds on annual plans after 14 days. Monthly plans can be canceled but you don't get prorated refunds. Test with free tier before committing to paid.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What about the annual discount?">
Annual saves $240/year ($79/month vs $99/month). But you're locked in for 12 months. Only worth it if you're certain you'll use ATP all year. Most people should start monthly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How does Apify usage pricing work?">
You pay for compute time (how long actors run). Typical costs for 100 ATP searches: $12-15/month. Includes free tier to start. Scales with actual usage, not users.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a way to share ATP Pro among team members?">
No. ATP is strictly single-user. Sharing login credentials violates terms of service. Each team member needs their own $99/month subscription.
</Accordion>
