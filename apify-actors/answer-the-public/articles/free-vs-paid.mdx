# Answer the Public Free vs Paid: Don't Pay Until You Read This

I've used Answer the Public's free tier, paid for Pro at $99/month, and eventually built my own solution because both options frustrated me.

Here's what you actually get at each level, and what most people get wrong about the upgrade decision.

## The Free Plan Truth

You get 3 searches per day. That's the whole story.

Everything else works the same as paid:
- Same data sources (Google, YouTube, TikTok, etc.)
- Same visualizations
- Same CSV exports
- Same country/language options

The only limit: 3 searches, then you wait 24 hours.

### When Free Actually Works

I used the free plan for months when I was just doing occasional client research. Three searches per day covered:
- Monday: Main product keyword
- Tuesday: Competitor keyword
- Wednesday: Industry term

Exported the CSVs, validated volumes in Ahrefs, planned content. Worked fine.

### When Free Breaks Down

The moment you need to do actual keyword research at scale, free becomes impossible.

Real scenario: Client wants content calendar for Q1
- Need to research: 50 keywords minimum
- Free plan timeline: 17 days
- Problem: Quarter starts in 2 weeks

You can't build a business on "wait 17 days for keyword research."

## What $99/Month Gets You

I paid for Pro for 8 months. Here's what actually changed:

**What you get:**
- Unlimited searches (real value)
- Search history (barely useful)
- Priority support (never needed it)
- Comparison mode (nice but not essential)

**What you don't get:**
- Search volume data (still need Ahrefs)
- Keyword difficulty scores (still need Ahrefs)
- API access (that's Expert tier only)
- Any automation whatsoever

The upgrade is really just "remove the 3 search limit." That's $99/month for unlimited searches.

## The Real Cost Breakdown

Let me show you the actual math based on how I used ATP:

**Month 1 with Pro:**
- Searched: 127 keywords
- Cost per search: $0.78
- Also paying for Ahrefs: $129/month
- Total SEO tools: $228/month

**Month 2 with Pro:**
- Searched: 43 keywords (slower month)
- Cost per search: $2.30
- Still need Ahrefs: $129/month
- Total: $228/month

You're paying $99/month whether you search 5 keywords or 500. And you still need volume data from somewhere else.

## What Nobody Tells You About Pro

### The CSV Export Hassle

Even with Pro, the workflow sucks:
1. Search keyword
2. Wait for results
3. Click export
4. Clean up CSV formatting
5. Remove duplicate suggestions
6. Filter nonsensical combinations
7. Repeat for next keyword

This takes 3-4 minutes per keyword. For 100 keywords, that's 5-6 hours of manual work.

At $50/hour labor cost, you're adding $250-300 in time to your $99 subscription.

Real monthly cost: ~$350-400 when you factor in labor.

### The Team Scaling Problem

Pro is single-user. Need to share with your team?

- 3 person team: $297/month
- 5 person team: $495/month
- 10 person agency: $990/month

For an agency, that's $11,880/year for keyword research data that doesn't even include search volumes.

## The Expert Plan ($199/Month)

I never paid for Expert. Here's why:

**What Expert adds:**
- Team features (still per-user pricing though)
- Brand monitoring
- White-label reports
- Limited API access

**What it costs:**
- $199/month ($2,388/year)
- At that price, you're competing with Ahrefs which gives you EVERYTHING

Ahrefs at $129/month gives you:
- Keyword research with volumes
- Competitor analysis
- Backlink data
- Rank tracking
- Site audits
- Content gap analysis

Answer the Public Expert at $199/month gives you:
- Question research
- Brand monitoring
- Pretty reports

The math doesn't math.

## When Should You Actually Pay?

I'm not saying never pay for ATP. But be honest about what you need:

### Stick with Free If:

- You research fewer than 5 keywords per month
- You're doing personal projects
- You have unlimited time
- You just need occasional inspiration

### Pay for Pro ($99/mo) If:

- You need more than 3 searches daily
- The visual presentations matter for clients
- You research 20-50 keywords monthly
- You don't want to deal with automation
- Budget isn't constrained

### Skip Expert ($199/mo) Unless:

- You specifically need white-label reports
- Brand monitoring is critical
- You've exhausted every other option
- Money truly isn't an object

### Use Automation If:

- You research 50+ keywords monthly
- You have multiple team members
- You integrate data with other tools
- You want to save $900+/year
- You value your time

## The Decision Framework

Ask yourself three questions:

**1. How many keywords do I research monthly?**
- < 10: Free might work
- 10-50: Pro or automation
- 50+: Automation makes sense

**2. Do I need the specific ATP interface?**
- Yes: Consider Pro
- No: Automation is cheaper

**3. What's my time worth?**
- If manual work costs more than automation, automate
- If you have unlimited time, use free

## What I Wish I Knew Earlier

### 1. The Data Isn't Proprietary

ATP doesn't have special access to secret keyword data. It's querying public APIs that you can access yourself.

The value is organization and presentation. You're paying for convenience, not unique data.

### 2. You'll Need Another Tool Anyway

ATP without volume data is incomplete. You'll end up paying for:
- ATP: $99/month
- Ahrefs/SEMrush: $129-140/month
- Total: $228-239/month

You're paying $100/month for question organization on top of your real SEO tool.

### 3. The Manual Workflow Doesn't Scale

Three months in, I realized I was spending 6-8 hours per month on manual exports and data cleaning.

That's $300-400 in labor costs added to the $99 subscription.

Real cost: $400-500/month when you count time.
