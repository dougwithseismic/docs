## Free Plan: What You Actually Get

### Search Limit Reality

<Warning>
The free plan gives you **1 search per day** (not 3 as it was previously). That's it. One keyword, once per day, then you wait 24 hours.
</Warning>

**What you can do with 1 search/day:**

- Research 1 keyword daily
- Export CSV data for that keyword
- View all data sources (Google, YouTube, TikTok, etc.)
- Download visualization images
- Access search volume estimates

**What you can't do:**

- Bulk keyword research
- Competitive analysis (multiple competitors)
- Content calendar planning (needs many keywords)
- Trend monitoring (requires regular re-searches)
- Client work (agencies need many searches)
- Product research at scale (e-commerce)

### The Math

Let's be realistic about what 1 search per day means:

**Scenario**: You want to build a content calendar for Q1 (3 months)

- Ideal keywords to research: 50-100
- Time needed with free plan: 50-100 days
- That's 1.5-3+ months just to do keyword research

**Scenario**: Agency managing 5 clients

- Each client needs 10 keywords researched monthly
- Total needed: 50 searches/month
- Time with free plan: 50 days (can't service clients)

**Scenario**: E-commerce launching product line

- 20 products, each needs keyword research
- Time with free plan: 20 days minimum
- Product launch delayed by weeks

### What's Actually Free

The free tier is essentially a **demo** of Answer the Public. It's useful for:

✅ Understanding how the tool works
✅ Testing with 1-2 keywords
✅ Evaluating if it's worth upgrading
✅ Very occasional, casual research

It's not viable for:

❌ Professional SEO work
❌ Content planning at scale
❌ Agency client work
❌ Competitive analysis
❌ Regular use

## Pro Plan: $99/Month ($79/month annual)

### What You Get

**Unlimited searches** - The main upgrade. Search as many keywords as you want, as often as you want.

**Additional features**:

- Historical comparison data
- Search alerts (notifications when search behavior changes)
- Priority support
- Data exports in CSV
- All visualization tools
- All data sources

### Who It's For

The Pro plan makes sense if you:

- Need more than 1 search daily (obviously)
- Do regular keyword research
- Manage multiple content properties
- Run a solo SEO/content business
- Want to avoid daily search limits

### The Hidden Costs

**$99/month = $1,188/year**

Or with annual discount: **$79/month = $948/year**

For one user, this might be reasonable. But consider:

**Team of 3**: $297/month or $3,564/year
**Agency with 5 users**: $495/month or $5,940/year

Even solo practitioners need to ask: "Will I get $1,200/year of value from unlimited keyword research?"

### What You Still Can't Do

Even with Pro, you're limited by manual workflows:

- ❌ No API access (can't automate)
- ❌ No webhooks (can't integrate with tools)
- ❌ No bulk processing (search one keyword at a time)
- ❌ No scheduled monitoring (must manually check)
- ❌ Manual data export (copy-paste to spreadsheets)

## Expert Plan: $199/Month ($149/month annual)

### What You Get

Everything in Pro, plus:

- **Team features**: Multiple user seats
- **Brand monitoring**: Track brand mentions
- **Advanced alerts**: More sophisticated notification rules
- **Priority support**: Faster response times
- **Comparison features**: Enhanced competitive analysis

### Who It's For

The Expert plan is designed for:

- Teams and agencies
- Brands monitoring reputation
- Companies doing serious competitive intelligence
- Organizations needing collaboration features

### The Cost Reality

**$199/month = $2,388/year**

Or annual: **$149/month = $1,788/year**

At this price point, you're competing with comprehensive SEO tools like:

- **Ahrefs**: $129-999/month (includes backlinks, site audits, rank tracking)
- **SEMrush**: $139-499/month (all-in-one SEO platform)
- **Moz Pro**: $99-599/month (complete SEO toolkit)

Answer the Public's Expert plan gives you question research and brand monitoring. Other tools give you that plus comprehensive SEO capabilities.
