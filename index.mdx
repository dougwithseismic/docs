Over the past 10 years, I've built production systems for companies like Contra, Groupon, Motley Fool, Sky, and MIT. I combine full-stack engineering with growth, product, and marketing experience to build software that drives revenue.

# What I've Built

**Contra's Indy.ai - Chrome Extension for Crowdsourced Job Discovery**
Built a [Chrome extension](/build/web-extensions) that turned 35,000+ Contra users into a distributed opportunity-finding network. The extension passively analyzes LinkedIn and X content while users browse, using [LLM workflows](/build/llm-workflows) to identify hiring signals. Result: 2x increase in marketplace inventory without hiring a single outbound recruiter.

- 30,000+ active users on Chrome Web Store
- 4.6 star rating
- Doubled job marketplace inventory
- Built with Plasmo, React, service workers, and LLM integration

[Read the full case study →](/case-studies/contra-linkedin-automation)

**Vouchernaut - Programmatic PPC Platform**
Built an [automated PPC platform](/articles/programmatic-acquisition-playbook) managing 100,000+ brands across affiliate networks. The system automatically generated campaigns, managed bids, and scaled to drive 60% of VoucherCloud's revenue. Contributed to their eventual Groupon acquisition.

- Managed £2M+ annual ad spend
- 100,000+ brands automated
- Drove 60% of company revenue
- Scaled from side project to $100k ARR before acquisition

[Read the full case study →](/case-studies/vouchernaut-programmatic-ppc)
