# Full-Stack Engineer with Product & Growth Experience

I'm Doug Silkstone, a British software engineer based in Prague. Over the past 10 years, I've built production systems for companies like Contra, Groupon, Motley Fool, Sky, and MIT. I've also built and sold three of my own products.

I combine full-stack engineering with growth, product, and marketing experience. This rare blend comes from years of building revenue-generating software - first as a bootstrapped founder, then as Head of Growth managing multi-million dollar budgets, and now as an engineer building systems that directly impact business metrics.

**My background:**

- Built my first business in 2016, scaled to $100k ARR, and sold it twice
- Worked across engineering, growth, and product with teams at Contra, Sky, Groupon, The Motley Fool, and MIT
- Helped early-stage startups collectively raise $20M+ USD
- Course contributor for MIT's Generative AI & LLM course
- Previous Head of Growth, managing systems that drove $10M+ in spend and over 1 billion product sessions
- Open-source contributor to TanStack, Mastra, and various tools

**What makes me different:**

I don't just write code - I understand how software drives revenue. Whether it's building Chrome extensions that double marketplace inventory, creating AI workflows that process thousands of hours of content, or architecting MVPs that scale to thousands of users, I focus on business impact.

On YouTube ([@dougsilkstone](https://www.youtube.com/@dougsilkstone)), I teach practical operator engineering - how to use code to create revenue. I'm an early adopter of Cursor/Claude Code and agentic workflows, and I share what I learn as I build.

# What I've Built

**Contra's Indy.ai - Chrome Extension for Crowdsourced Job Discovery**
Built a Chrome extension that turned 35,000+ Contra users into a distributed opportunity-finding network. The extension passively analyzes LinkedIn and X content while users browse, using LLMs to identify hiring signals. Result: 2x increase in marketplace inventory without hiring a single outbound recruiter.

- 30,000+ active users on Chrome Web Store
- 4.6 star rating
- Doubled job marketplace inventory
- Built with Plasmo, React, service workers, and LLM integration

**Vouchernaut - Programmatic PPC Platform**
Built an automated PPC platform managing 100,000+ brands across affiliate networks. The system automatically generated campaigns, managed bids, and scaled to drive 60% of VoucherCloud's revenue. Contributed to their eventual Groupon acquisition.

- Managed £2M+ annual ad spend

- Scaled from side project to $100k ARR before acquisition
