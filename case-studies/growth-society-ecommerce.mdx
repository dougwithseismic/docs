## The Opportunity

Will Anderson at Growth Society reached out with a compelling vision: build a follow-up to their wildly popular Growth Signals report - but as an interactive platform.

The original report was a static PDF that ranked the UK's fastest-growing e-commerce brands. It generated significant industry attention. The question was: could we transform this into a living, breathing benchmarking tool?

## The Sprint

We committed to a **4-week MVP sprint** with clear deliverables:

**Week 1**: Data architecture and ingestion pipeline
**Week 2**: Core dashboard and ranking engine
**Week 3**: Brand profiles and comparison tools
**Week 4**: Polish, performance, and launch

## The Impact

The platform transformed a static annual report into a dynamic resource that:

- **Drives Traffic**: Brands share their rankings, creating organic distribution
- **Generates Leads**: Download prompts capture interested e-commerce operators
- **Establishes Authority**: Growth Society positioned as the definitive source for UK e-commerce data
- **Creates Partnerships**: Brands reach out to be featured or to access deeper data

## Why It Matters

E-commerce benchmarking is notoriously opaque. Most brands operate in isolation, with no visibility into how their growth compares to peers. Growth Society's platform changes that, providing the industry transparency that drives better decision-making.
