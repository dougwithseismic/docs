## The Brief

Communicate cutting-edge science without alienating non-technical visitors.

Helix, an AI-powered protein folding research platform, needed a landing page that would resonate with two very different audiences:

1. **Researchers**: Scientists who need to trust the platform's credibility
2. **Investors**: Business stakeholders who need to understand the opportunity

The visual language needed to feel scientifically credible and distinctly modern.

## Design Approach

### Visual Language

We developed a design system that bridges science and technology:

- **Color Palette**: Deep blues and purples evoking molecular visualization software
- **Typography**: Clean, technical typefaces that feel at home in academic papers
- **Imagery**: Abstract 3D representations of protein structures
- **Motion**: Subtle animations that suggest molecular movement

### Content Strategy

The page guides visitors through:

1. **The Problem**: Why protein folding matters for drug discovery
2. **The Solution**: How AI accelerates the research process
3. **The Technology**: Technical credibility without overwhelming jargon
4. **The Team**: Scientific credentials that build trust
