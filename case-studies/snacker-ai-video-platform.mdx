## The Challenge

GrowthMatch, a TechStars alumni company, runs a done-for-you video content concierge service. They wanted to productize their expertise using LLMs—creating an AI-powered video editing platform that could compete with established tools like Riverside and Descript.

The technical challenge: How do you teach an LLM to edit video from transcript text alone—no visual or audio cues—while maintaining natural flow and adapting to individual speaking styles? The goal was to deliver polished, edited videos in under 20 seconds, eliminating the traditional editing interface entirely.

## Building the Solution

The partnership began in the Cursor forums. As a YouTube creator, the problem resonated immediately: producing video content at scale without sacrificing quality. We inherited a Lovable prototype and incrementally refactored it to production standards (monorepo, Hono backend, proper Supabase integration) while maintaining shipping velocity.

## Technical Implementation

### Tech Stack

- **Architecture**: TypeScript monorepo
- **Frontend**: Vite + React
- **Backend**: Hono framework
- **Database**: Supabase
- **Video Processing**: Web Codecs API + HLS playlist generation
- **AI**: LLM APIs for content generation and editing

### Browser-Based Video Challenges

Browser video editing is technically challenging. You're working with hardware limitations, viewport constraints, and browser security models. We evaluated Remotion, WebCodecs API, and Diffusion Studio.

**Client vs. Server Rendering**

- Client rendering is cheaper and faster, but dependent on user hardware
- Server rendering provides consistency but requires expensive infrastructure
- Mobile presents additional constraints—limited processing power and screen size

**iOS Safari Audio Blocking**

iOS Safari blocks audio autoplay when stitching videos—a security feature that breaks traditional video editing workflows. Our solution:

- Created HLS playlists instead of traditional video stitching
- Segmented videos into timestamp-based chunks
- Built custom playlist sections to bypass iOS restrictions
- Trade-off: Lost Remotion's advanced animation capabilities

**Dual-Stream Processing**

We record two streams simultaneously:
- Full resolution for final output
- Lightweight proxy for real-time transcription

This approach balances speed with quality—users get near-instant transcription without sacrificing final video quality.

## LLM-Powered Video Editing

### The Core Innovation

Teaching an LLM to edit video from transcripts alone presented a unique challenge. The AI needs to understand context and intent from text—no visual or audio cues. Everyone has different speaking styles and pacing preferences.

This put us in direct competition with well-funded, established players like Riverside and Descript—but with a fundamentally different approach: fully automated editing based on LLM transcript analysis, eliminating the editing interface entirely.

### Training the AI

The breakthrough came through internal testing and systematic evals:

- Deliberately tried to "trip up" the AI with edge cases
- Used the product for all our own content creation
- Introduced systematic evaluation frameworks
- Iterated until the internal team couldn't break it

When we couldn't break the AI editing anymore, we knew it was ready for users.

### Three-Layer AI Integration

**1. Content Ideation**
- AI-generated talking points and prompts
- Topic suggestions based on audience and goals
- Session planning and content calendars

**2. Automatic Video Editing**
- Removes gaps, pauses, and mistakes automatically
- Creates coherent takes from raw recordings
- Accepts feedback for re-editing
- Produces polished videos in 20 seconds
- Adapts to individual speaking styles through context understanding

**3. Content Distribution**
- Generates platform-specific captions and descriptions
- One-click publishing to LinkedIn, X, YouTube
- Creates blog posts and articles from video content

## Technical Learnings

### Browser-Based Video Processing

WebCodecs API and tools like Remotion are advancing client-side video processing. Browser-based video editing can now rival native applications in capability, though trade-offs remain around hardware dependencies and mobile constraints.

### LLM-Based Editing

Key insights from training an LLM to edit video:

- Context understanding from transcripts alone is achievable with systematic training
- Individual speaking style adaptation requires extensive evaluation frameworks
- Internal dogfooding is essential—if your team can't break it, users probably won't either
- 20-second processing time creates a fundamentally different UX than traditional editing tools

### Architecture

Starting from AI-generated prototypes requires careful refactoring:

- Monorepo structure enables shared patterns and components
- Incremental refactoring maintains velocity while improving foundation
- Clear guardrails let non-technical founders continue shipping features
- Production LLM applications need proper API key management, caching, and rate limiting
