## The Philosophy: Operator Engineering

Traditional engineering is about "correctness" and "clean code".
**Operator Engineering** is about **leverage**.

- **Traditional Dev**: "I need 3 weeks to refactor the authentication service."
- **Operator Engineer**: "I can use a managed auth provider and ship the billing feature today."

We optimize for **Time to Revenue**. We believe that the best code is the code that is running in production, solving a user's problem.

## Why We Don't Do "Agile"

We don't do daily standups. We don't do Jira tickets. We don't do estimation poker.
These are rituals designed to manage low-trust teams.

We operate on **High Trust / High Velocity**:

1.  **Weekly Sync**: We align on the business goal for the week.
2.  **Async Execution**: We build, ship, and demo via Loom/Slack.
3.  **Direct Access**: You talk to the engineer building your product, not a manager.
