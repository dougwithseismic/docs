## Key Elements to Customize

1. **Opening line**: Pick most relevant case study
2. **Project alignment**: Connect their needs to your experience
3. **Timeline**: Be specific and realistic
4. **Tech stack**: Mention if they specified technologies
5. **Call to action**: Portfolio link or availability

## Keep It Short

- 3-5 sentences max
- Lead with results, not process
- Specific metrics > generic claims
- Always include portfolio link
